02 / THE LORE

The Return of Toad

The origin story, as told by the Toad community.

  1. 1988

    Before the feed.

    The community traces El Toad Pepe to Carlitos Balá’s Argentine children’s television show: a green felt toad, red shirt, yellow suspenders.

  2. 2005

    The internet frog.

    The community’s timeline contrasts Toad’s earlier TV story with Matt Furie’s introduction of Pepe in Boy’s Club.

  3. 2013

    A childhood anthem.

    The community credits El Reino Infantil and La Granja de Zenón with bringing the character’s song to a new audience.

  4. 2023

    Frogs find crypto.

    The community places the Pepe memecoin wave in the next chapter of its story.

  5. 2026

    The pond goes on-chain.

    The community brings the character to Solana as $TOAD. A new chapter for a very familiar face.
