TOAD / Paper Pulse — community creator starter kit

Character: Keep the green plush texture, oversized expressive eyes, crooked smile, red shirt, and yellow overalls with suspenders. Preserve proportions across shots.
Voice: Relatable, a little chaotic, warm, and never mean. Everyday moments work best. Same Toad. Different days.
Palette: Cream #F9F1D7; forest #144137; green #35D391; yellow #F6BE2D; lime #B5DB3A; red #E02B2F.
Type: Anton for display; DM Sans for body; IBM Plex Mono for labels; Caveat for short handwritten notes. Font files are not bundled.
Layout: Give the character breathing room. Keep captions readable, short, and within video safe areas. Use forest text on cream, yellow, lime, or green.
Video: Start with the attached script. Attach the references to your AI tool. Add captions separately when the generator cannot render clean text. Use audio you have permission to use.
Community content: Do not present a fan video as an official announcement or make price predictions or promises.

Included: supplied front reference, face reference, rear reference, pose reference sheet, creator guidelines, and AI video prompt.
A complete all-angle turnaround pack is not available yet. These are visual references, not a 3D model.
