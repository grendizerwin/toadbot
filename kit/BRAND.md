## 9. Character: what Toad looks like

From the site: "El Toad Pepe: green plush, red tee, yellow suspenders." "Real plush texture. Lime skin, red tee, yellow straps." Video character lock: "green plush texture, oversized eyes, crooked stitched smile, red shirt, yellow overalls with suspenders. The head is a solid, rigid costume piece, and the eyes and mouth never move."

From my own look at the reference images:
- **Format:** a full-body **plush mascot costume**, photographed for real (not a cartoon or illustration). It has adult human proportions with a very large head, about 1/3 of total height.
- **Skin:** yellow-green / lime **fuzzy short-pile plush fur**, close to `#B5DB3A` with olive shading, with a visible fabric nap. Arms, hands, legs, and feet are the same plush.
- **Head:** a wide, rounded, slightly squashed frog head that is wider than it is tall. Two soft bumps on top hold the eyes. A faint vertical seam runs down the center of the head (visible from the back).
- **Eyes:** two large glossy **white domed eyes with big black pupils and a small white catchlight**. Heavy plush **half-closed upper lids / brows** cover the top of each eye and give a sleepy, smug, knowing look. The eyes sit slightly asymmetric: the pupils glance sideways or toward the viewer, and one eye sits a bit lower or bigger.
- **Mouth:** a single long, thin **black stitched smile** curving across the face, slightly crooked or asymmetric. No teeth, no tongue, and no open mouth in any official reference.
- **No** nose, nostrils, ears, or eyebrows separate from the lids.
- **Outfit:** a bright **red short-sleeve crew-neck t-shirt** (about `#E02B2F` to deep red), **mustard/golden-yellow shorts-overalls** (knee-length, about `#F6BE2D`), and **yellow suspender straps** fastened at the front waist with **round white buttons sewn with a red "x" stitch**. From the back the straps **cross in an X**, with two white buttons at the waistband. The back-view banner (`toad-back`) shows a small cream tag that says "toad" on the back of the shorts.
- **Hands:** chunky four-fingered plush hands. **Feet:** big flat plush frog feet with rounded toes.
- **Expression range:** always the same fixed face (rigid costume head). Emotion comes from body pose, props, and setting. The default read is relaxed, smug, deadpan contentment ("Currently doing his best").
- **Official poses** (pose sheet): **Waving**, **Arms crossed**, **Holding a coin** (a gold coin with a smile), **Cheering** (both arms up). Other poses on the site: sitting on a small wooden stool ("off duty"), standing alone, turning away from camera, taking a selfie, toasting.
- **Photography style of official references:** clean studio shots on flat Toad-green (`#35D391`-ish) or cream backgrounds, soft even light, soft floor shadow. The meme versions place the same plush suit into photoreal scenes (space, a burning city, an empty pool, a tuxedo event, a psychedelic tunnel).

## 12. Do's and don'ts

From the site and kit (verbatim sources above):
- DO keep the plush texture, green skin, red tee, yellow straps and overalls, and expressive oversized eyes. Preserve proportions across shots.
- DO let the situation change while keeping Toad recognizable. Everyday moments work best.
- DO keep the head rigid in video: eyes and mouth never move, and the body does the acting.
- DO give the character breathing room. Keep captions short, readable, and inside safe areas. Add captions separately if the generator can't render clean text.
- DO use forest text on cream, yellow, lime, or green. Use red only as a small accent.
- DO tell people to verify the full contract address.
- DO use only audio you have rights to.
- DON'T be mean. The voice is "never mean."
- DON'T present fan content as an official announcement.
- DON'T make price predictions or promises ("no promise of financial returns").
- DON'T assume commercial-use rights. The kit "do[es] not supply a commercial-use license". Contact @thetoadmeme_.
- **[inferred]** Don't redraw Toad as a flat cartoon or as Pepe. The official look is a real photographed plush suit. Don't give him an open mouth, teeth, or a moving face.
