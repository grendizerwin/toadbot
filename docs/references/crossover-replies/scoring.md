# Scoring crossover windows

Score **remixability + author-notice + taste**. CT adjacency is not required. Crypto adjacency is a fail.

## Hard fail

- Parent is about coins, charts, listings, wallets, pads, or CT personalities talking bags
- No usable still (text-only, or video with no freeze-frame)
- the owner already replied to that author / thread in 24–48h
- Same remix scene as the last pack
- Minors, sexual content that could involve a minor, suicide, crime victims, mass-casualty wreckage
- Giveaways, follow-trains, engagement pods
- Trolls, scams, copycat contracts, or any target where the only joke mocks a person or another coin
- Caption or remix that matches one already used in this pack or the last 48h
- @thetoadmeme_ already replied under that parent post
- Photo of a private individual, a logo the remix would turn into a fake endorsement, or an author who asked not to be edited
- Official government emergency warning where a joke would look like impersonation of an alert (a routine outlook map can pass; a missing-person or tornado-warning-right-now post does not)

## Author-notice (need two)

Huge list accounts bury comments. Compensate with speed and a distinct image.

- Posted in the last 1–4 hours, or replies still arriving
- Reply count still walkable (prefer under ~120; stretch to ~250 only if the post is under ~90 minutes old)
- Parent image is simple enough that a remake is readable in a reply thumbnail
- Author or page historically likes / replies to meme comments
- Views already proving the post will travel (rough floor a few thousand, no hard cap)

A 4M-view weather post with 1,800 replies is usually a skip unless you would be commenting in the first hour. The weather-map example worked as a *format*, not as a "always reply under 4M views" rule.

## Remixability (need one strong)

- Clear hero object or layout to occupy
- Photo or graphic, not a wall of text
- Joke is obvious without knowing $TOAD
- Official plush will still look like the official plush in that lighting

## Taste (need pass)

Would a normal person who follows that page find the comment funny rather than spammy or cruel.

## Score 1–5

- **5** — Fresh visual, list author, early thread, obvious drop-in, author might tap it
- **4** — Good visual, still visible, joke is clean
- **3** — Visual works but burial risk is real. Optional third only
- **2** — Late, weak image, or needs a caption thesis. Skip
- **1** — Crypto, tasteless, no image, already used. Skip

## Pack rules

- Two or three different authors
- Different joke classes
- At most three image replies
- Empty pack beats three forced frogs on charts
