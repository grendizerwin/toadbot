# Caption voice — a pond member, not a brand intern

Brand voice (creator kit, verbatim): "Relatable, a little chaotic, warm, and never mean. Everyday moments work best. Same Toad. Different days." See BRAND.md §3 and §5.

The poster (the owner) is a member of the pond, not the project. Never write as if it's an official announcement.

Live official lines worth matching in length: "Haters will say this is AI 🐸", "Croak intensifies", "Let's get this bread 🐸". Approved batch 001 posts are in `viral-hooks.md`.

The fail is a caption that explains the character, the thesis, or the market.

## House voice

- Lowercase-friendly. Short. One beat, two if the joke needs a setup, three only for quote/time-skip.
- Start from an everyday moment (monday, one more meeting, sunday night, weekend, "one quick thing").
- Warm and a little chaotic. Never mean, never a dunk on another coin or person.
- Name what's in the image, or let the image finish the line.
- $TOAD at most once, usually last. Often the QT already carries it.
- Under 280. Default 40–140.
- Site vocabulary used lightly: the pond, tomorrow problem, having a day, currently doing his best, no perfect days required, same toad. different days.

## Hard bans

- Price predictions, "pump", moon, targets, "next 100x", financial advice, promises, FOMO
- The contract address unless the user asked (then add "verify the full address")
- Anything that reads like an official announcement
- Mean jokes, dunks on other coins, holders, or people
- Recycled lore from `uniqueness.md`
- Slogan couplets, TED closers, triple stacked declaratives
- Quietly one of the most / thesis / potential / volatility sermon
- Chatbot residue: here's the thing, let's, I hope this helps, worth noting
- Fake memoir, invented PnL
- Empty LFG / wagmi / "who else is here"
- Bulk tagging; more than one @ per post
- Explaining El Sapo Pepe to a stranger when the image already is the character

## Allowed

- Light "ngl / lmao / wait so / i'd"
- One 🐸
- A slash-command gag only if it's new and has nothing to do with price
- No closer

## Sound checks

1. **Situation test** — does the first line name a real moment or object? If not, rewrite.
2. **Photo test** — cover the image. If the caption still works for any frog coin, rewrite. (Brand phrases like "tomorrow problem" or "the pond" plus the plush in the scene count as TOAD-specific.)
3. **Official test** — if your caption is three times longer than @thetoadmeme_'s, cut it.
4. **Phone test** — thumb-typed, not drafted.
5. **Kindness test** — would anyone in the reply section feel mocked? Rewrite.

## After avoid-ai-writing

Cleanup only. Then re-loosen. Keep the fragment, the lowercase, the turn. Throw away any sentence that "completes the thought." If the cleaned caption is stiffer than the approved batch 001 posts, cut it again.
