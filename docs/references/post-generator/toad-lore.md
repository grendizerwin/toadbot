# $TOAD / El Toad Pepe Lore (background, not daily copy)

## Origin (community lore, not established fact)

Frame every origin line the way the site does: "the community traces…". BRAND.md §4 is the source.

- 1988 (site): "The community traces El Toad Pepe to Carlitos Balá's Argentine children's television show: a green felt toad, red shirt, yellow suspenders."
- The @thetoadmeme_ X bio credits the original character "El Sapo Pepe" to Analía García in 1988. Treat that as community lore too; don't state it as fact or write that she "created" the $TOAD mascot.
- 2005 (site): the community's timeline contrasts Toad's earlier TV story with Matt Furie's introduction of Pepe in Boy's Club. Chronology is the joke. Never claim Furie copied it or that $TOAD owns the character.
- 2013 (site): the community credits El Reino Infantil and La Granja de Zenón with bringing the song to a new audience. Their 3D orange-eyed farm frog is NOT the $TOAD mascot.
- 2023–2026 (site): Pepe memecoin wave, then "the pond goes on-chain" as $TOAD on Solana.
- The site describes an independent community project with no endorsement from the original character rights holders.

## Visual

The 1988 TV character was felt; the $TOAD mascot is plush. See visual-bible.md. Do not generate the El Reino Infantil 3D frog.
