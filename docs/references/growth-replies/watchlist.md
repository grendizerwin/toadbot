# Accounts that can help popularize $TOAD

This is a starting map, not a hall of fame. Every run must replace static names with **who actually posted in this window**. If a listed account is silent, drop them from "Accounts that help today."

## Project

- `@thetoadmeme_` — only official (https://x.com/thetoadmeme_). Source of truth for media. Weak reply destination (community already there). Best as QT fuel or as the subject of someone else's post.
- `@thetoadmcme__` and `@thetoadmeme_1` — unofficial lookalikes. Do not treat as official. Do not amplify their posts as project media.

## High-leverage humans (only when they are live)

These matter when they mention the character, a mascot, Argentine culture, or quote official media. Silent names do not belong in the pack.

- People who already touched TOAD in public (find them live; don't keep a fixed roster). Re-check live. Do not @ them cold with a ticker dump.
- Mid-size Solana holders and meme accounts who post as themselves, not as pads.
- LatAm / Argentine CT accounts talking childhood songs, El Sapo, or Carlitos Balá — only if the post is public and adjacent.
- Artists posting plush / mascot work. Image replies can work here if you generate the official plush suit from the brand-kit references, not their art style.

When you name an account in the package, give a *this-window* reason: a post URL, a view count, a QT of official, a question they asked.

## How to pick "accounts that help today"

Prefer:

1. Posted in the last 8 hours
2. Real person, not a brand dashboard
3. Mid-tier reach (a reply can still be seen)
4. Already adjacent or already talking TOAD
5. History of replying to commenters

Avoid:

- Follow-back farms and collaboration-DM pages
- Launchpad / factory / protocol dashboards
- Impostor TOAD support accounts, copycat contracts, scam and troll accounts (never engage)
- Mega-accounts where a comment dies on page 12

## Follow-watch vs reply-now

- **Reply-now** — they have an open mid-size post this window.
- **Follow-watch** — they are adjacent but the current post is already buried or off-topic. Put them in Next scan watch, do not force a comment.
